HV leakage can happen in RPCs through corona and through sparks.

Corona is the same effect that makes the buzzling sound close to the urban tension lines. It's a flow of electrons or ions (different types of corona) in air that doesn't overcome the breakout voltage so it does not make the air conductive enough to create a spark.

Also sparks can happen. For example in TOMAR, between the HV feedthrough and the grounding plane. It might have been possible that a spark also connected the HV feedthrough with the reading strip, which may have broken a FEE amplifier, which explains why a channel does not measure, not even with the antenna method to introduce noise in the channel.
