# MINGO01 self-trigger rate compared with IFIN-HH MSMGRPC prototypes

## Data and calculation

Compared the MINGO01 self-trigger Task 2 data with the reported IFIN-HH CBM MSMGRPC prototype dark rates. The analyzed output was `selftrigger_calibrated_mi0126284033542.parquet`, containing 48,248 events over a 121 s acquisition interval.

For the spatial rate map, calibrated Tdif was cut to `[-1.2, 1.2] ns`, converted to X using `199.8616 mm/ns`, and restricted to the physical 300 mm strip length. The four strips in each plane have a combined width of 357 mm, giving an active area of 1,071 cm² per plane. Counts were divided by each X-bin surface area and by acquisition time, so the surface-map values are in Hz/cm². Integrating the bin rates over area recovers the selected hit rate for each plane.

| Plane | Selected hits | Selected rate | Area-averaged rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 11,268 | 93.1 Hz | 0.087 Hz/cm² |
| 2 | 19,541 | 161.5 Hz | 0.151 Hz/cm² |
| 3 | 6,247 | 51.6 Hz | 0.048 Hz/cm² |
| 4 | 13,065 | 108.0 Hz | 0.101 Hz/cm² |
| **Four-plane area-weighted average** | **50,121** | **414.2 Hz** | **0.097 Hz/cm²** |

## Reference comparison and limits

The IFIN-HH presentation reports dark rates of 0.11 and 0.14 Hz/cm² for two MSMGRPC prototypes, with dark currents below 1 nA. The presentation describes 16 operated strips, an operated area of 86.6 cm² per prototype, and a 90% C₂H₂F₄ + 5% SF₆ + 5% isobutane gas mixture. See the [IFIN-HH MSMGRPC presentation](https://agenda.infn.it/event/22092/contributions/167316/attachments/91578/124391/HR_HG_MSMGRPC.pdf).

Numerically, the MINGO01 area-averaged rate is of the same order: 0.097 Hz/cm² is about 12% below 0.11 Hz/cm² and 31% below 0.14 Hz/cm². Plane 2 is near the upper reference, while the other planes are lower.

This is a provisional comparison, not a direct measurement of intrinsic dark rate. The MINGO value is derived from a self-trigger event stream after the stated Tdif and physical-position cuts; trigger acceptance, threshold, HV, gas, and test conditions are not matched to the prototype measurement. The MINGO DAQ data also do not provide the dark-current measurement needed to compare the reported `<1 nA` values.
